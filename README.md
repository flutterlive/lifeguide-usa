# LifeGuide USA — Simple Solutions for Everyday Life

A professional American problem-solving platform with practical, step-by-step guides for everyday life in the USA.

## What is included
- Homepage with search: "What do you need help with today?"
- 8 categories with 47 topics: Money & Saving, Jobs & Career, Home & Housing, Moving & Relocation, Family & Daily Life, Technology & Digital Life, Car & Transportation, Emergency & Safety
- 10 full detailed guides with FAQs and official .gov source links
- 7 browser-based tools: Monthly Budget Planner (50/30/20), Moving Planner, Emergency Planner, Daily Checklist, 52-Week Saving Challenge, Home Maintenance Calendar, Job Search Planner
- My Life Dashboard (saved on the user's own device)
- About, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Disclaimer pages
- Site-wide search, mobile-friendly design, American English

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a browser, or host it on any static host.

## Deploy on Vercel
Import this repository on Vercel as a static site (Other framework preset, no build command), with `index.html` as the entry point.
